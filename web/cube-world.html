How to move

Enter world, then use W/A/S/D to move. Drag to look, or use the arrow keys. Space or E rises; Q descends. Shift moves faster. Movement stays inside the 200 µm cube. Escape returns to overview. On a touch screen, use the movement buttons and drag the scene.

The overview camera is outside the cube; the explorer position is retained inside it. Initial/After switches between computed fields. It is not a time animation.
